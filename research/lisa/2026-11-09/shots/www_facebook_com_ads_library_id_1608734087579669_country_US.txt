What happens when technology investment meets a shortage of essential real estate?

Corvallis is one of just 31 federally designated Tech Hubs in the U.S., with a $45M EDA grant, a $53M CHIPS Act investment, and a $200M university innovation complex shaping the local economy.

At the same time, 58%+ of Corvallis households rent, creating ongoing demand associated with moves, downsizing, and household transitions.

Signal Ventures is developing an estimated 591-unit self-storage facility in Corvallis as part of the Innovation Corridor Fund.

Review the project, market fundamentals, and development strategy.
