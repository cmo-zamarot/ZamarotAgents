Webinar Description

The wait is over. After finishing due diligence and final deal structuring, the Truckee land entitlement opportunity is ready. In this webinar replay, we discuss the Truckee Hilltop project. It is an 8.05-acre land entitlement project in Truckee, CA, with a targeted IRR of 24% and a projected equity multiple of 2.15x over an anticipated 3-year hold. Full risk factors and deal structure are in the Offering Memorandum.
