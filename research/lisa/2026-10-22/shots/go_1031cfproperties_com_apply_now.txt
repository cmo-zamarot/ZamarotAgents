THE DEMOGRAPHIC TAILWIND

Why We Focus on Senior Housing

Senior housing is one of the most resilient and demand-driven asset classes in commercial real estate. The aging Baby Boomer population — the second-largest generation in the U.S. — is driving an unprecedented need for assisted living and memory care facilities. It is estimated that over 800,000 additional units of senior housing will be needed in the U.S. by 2030. Americans living with Alzheimer’s disease could more than double by 2050, reaching 13.8 million. Unlike discretionary real estate, senior housing is needs-based — residents rely on these facilities for essential care, providing inherent resilience across market cycles.

Recent independent analysis forecasts senior housing operated properties (SHOP) to deliver 11% NOI growth from 2024 to 2028 — outpacing virtually every other real estate sector.​
