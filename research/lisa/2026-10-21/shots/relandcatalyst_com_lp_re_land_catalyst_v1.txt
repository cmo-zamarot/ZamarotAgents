Straight Answers
Do I really need $0 of my own capital?
▼
Yes. When you source a qualified deal, we bring the capital to acquire the land and fund the entitlement process. You choose: get paid upfront or stay in as an equity partner.
I have no land development experience. Can I still do this?
▼
Absolutely. The program teaches entitlement from scratch. If you understand RE fundamentals deal analysis, negotiation, due diligence you already have most transferable skills. We teach the rest.
How long does a typical entitlement deal take?
▼
Most timelines run 3–18 months depending on the municipality and complexity, though simpler deals can close faster and more complex rezoning can extend beyond that. Each deal can generate up to $10k - $50k finders fee.
What's the risk if an entitlement gets denied?
▼
Smart deal selection dramatically reduces denial risk. Our AI tool and analysis process pre-qualify parcels before committing. We focus on growth corridors with favorable zoning trajectories not speculative long shots.
How is this different from land flipping?
▼
Night and day. Land flippers buy cheap rural lots for marginal gains. Land entitlement is a development strategy you create value by getting land approved for a higher use. That's why margins are 2–3x, not 20%. You're operating upstream of billion-dollar pipelines.
