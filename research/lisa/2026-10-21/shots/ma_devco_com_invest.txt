INVESTMENT DETAILS
How the Fund Works
FUND STRATEGY
Acquire & Entitle

Capital is deployed into strategic land positions within the Texas Triangle. Each asset undergoes entitlement, infrastructure planning, and site preparation to unlock value before delivery to builders.

CAPITAL DEPLOYMENT
Phased Deployment

Investments are called in tranches aligned with acquisition schedules. This phased approach matches capital to milestones, reducing idle cash and targeting optimal entry timing across submarkets.

EXIT & RETURNS
Builder Partnerships
