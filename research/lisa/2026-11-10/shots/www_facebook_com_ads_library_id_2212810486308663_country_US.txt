Sold an appreciated asset in 2026? The capital gains decision may not have ended at closing.

Many sellers treat the meeting with their accountant as the end of the tax question. For accredited investors it often is not. Once a gain is recognized, you generally have 180 days to invest it into a qualified opportunity zone offering, and a gain reported on a Schedule K-1 can start that clock later than you think.

Gains realized in 2026 can still receive OZ 2.0 treatment if the 180-day window runs into 2027. If you have a capital gain, the new opportunity zone program comes with a 5-year tax deferral, 10% step up in basis and 10-year tax-free appreciation.*
