WALTON GLOBAL
WALTON IS A GLOBAL REAL ESTATE ASSET MANAGEMENT COMPANY FOCUSED ON           PRE-DEVELOPMENT LAND IN THE PATH OF GROWTH.
Founded in 1979, Walton currently manages US $4.64 billion of real estate assets on behalf of investors from more than 91 countries. Walton’s investment approach has multiple phases, including identifying the right parcel of land for acquisition, syndicating the land through investment structures best suited for our various global sales channels, managing the land during its hold period and ultimately divesting of the land for a projected overall profit.
 
This way to hold, own, manage and monetize land assets makes us attractive to partner with best in class local, regional, and national developers and builders. Walton currently has more than 80,500 acres of land under ownership/management throughout North America, with 90% located across United States.
TRANSFORMING RAW LAND INTO SMART INVESTMENTS

For more than 47 years, Walton has created wealth opportunities through land investments for investors worldwide through institutional and retail channels.

IMPACT THAT SPANS THE GLOBE

Our real estate assets span across 34 regional markets, comprised of more than 198 master-planned communities, with multiple uses including residential, commercial and industrial purposes. Walton’s offices are located around the world, including Calgary, Phoenix, Hong Kong, Singapore, Taipei, Manila and Tokyo.

ATTAINABLE WEALTH FROM THE LAND EXPERTS

Walton’s investment strategy and growth principles are coupled with a global leadership team that has expertise across multiple real estate sectors.

WALTON’S MISSION STATEMENT
Walton will employ a land acquisition strategy and asset management model to generate revenue and profit for our global stakeholders and shareholders. We will work with best-in-class builders and developers to monetize our assets across North America.
